--- title: swarmwatch description: Live detector for LLM-swarm-like bursts in the public urlquery.net feed, re-scored every 15 minutes date: 2026-10-07 layout: project-app ---

Watches every public scan on urlquery.net for sites that suddenly get many new URLs, measured against each site's own 60-day baseline. Agent swarms show up this way because they enumerate pages, while monitors and phishing resubmits repeat the same URL. The data is live from my server and re-scored every 15 minutes. Example URLs are left out of this public copy.
